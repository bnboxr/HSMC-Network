# HSMC — Legal Foundation Pack

This folder contains **template** legal documents that MUST be reviewed
and customised by a crypto-specialised lawyer before any public
fundraising, listing, or token distribution. They are starting points,
not finished filings.

## Recommended structure

| Step | Action                                                              | Cost (EUR) |
| ---- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| 1    | Incorporate a Foundation (Switzerland Zug, BVI, or Estonia OÜ)      | 3–15k      |
| 2    | KYC for all 3 founders (notarised IDs)                              | 0.5k       |
| 3    | Legal opinion: "wHSMC is a utility token, not a security"          | 5–15k      |
| 4    | Terms of Service + Privacy Policy (GDPR + AMLD5 compliant)          | 2–5k       |
| 5    | Token sale agreement template (if private round)                    | 3k         |
| 6    | KYC/AML provider integration (Sumsub, Onfido, Persona)              | 1–3k setup + 1–3 EUR/user |
| 7    | MiCA registration (EU, mandatory from Dec 2024)                     | 10–50k     |

## Files in this folder

- `Terms_of_Service_TEMPLATE.md` — base ToS, **not legal advice**
- `Privacy_Policy_TEMPLATE.md` — GDPR-aligned, **not legal advice**
- `Token_Disclaimer.md` — risk disclosure shown on first wallet creation
- `Founder_Declaration_TEMPLATE.md` — signed statement of non-malicious intent
- `LEGAL_NOTICE.md` — public notice to display in app footer until lawyer signs off

Until step 3 (legal opinion) is signed:
- DO NOT accept fiat funds from users for tokens
- DO NOT call HSMC an "investment"
- DO NOT promise returns or staking yields above the on-chain mining reward
